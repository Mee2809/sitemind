The invoice always shows up. The answer never does.

Lifts get ordered by phone, tracked in a spreadsheet, and called off when somebody remembers. Weeks later the bill lands on the office desk and the only question anyone asks is who forgot.

  • 03

    Without a record, the super blames the office and the office blames the super.

05 / Straight answers

Questions contractors ask.

Do my crews need to download an app?

No. It's a web page that works on any phone. They save it to the home screen and it opens like an app.

Do rental companies have to sign up?

No. They get a normal text and email with the order, the address and the on-site contact. Same as a phone call, minus the phone tag.

We already use Procore or Viewpoint.

Those track projects and the equipment you own. Ask what happens when a rented lift is done: does anyone get told? SiteMind covers that gap, the rented stuff that lives in phone calls and spreadsheets.

What does the AI actually do?

It reads the request, turns it into a clean order, and flags emergencies or missing details. A person always reviews and confirms before anything is sent.

Rentals don't have an end date. Does that break it?

That's the point. Most rentals are open-ended, so SiteMind counts days on rent instead of chasing due dates. It's the number nobody tracks today.

Where does our data live?

In a shared table your office can filter, sort and export. Every request keeps who ordered it, when, for which job, and when it went off rent.
