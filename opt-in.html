SMS Opt-In

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Mauricio Ellis, operating as SiteMind, collects SMS consent through an online job request form used by field superintendents to request equipment rentals for their jobsites. Below is the exact field and consent language shown on that form.

Job Request Form — Assignee Phone Number Field

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive SMS text messages about your equipment rental status, including reminders and due date alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

How Opt-In Works

When a field superintendent submits a job request through this internal form, they enter their mobile phone number and confirm the request. By submitting the form with their phone number, they consent to receive SMS reminders about their active equipment rentals, including daily summaries and alerts when a rental's end date is approaching.

Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.
