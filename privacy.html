Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mauricio Ellis, operating as SiteMind ("we," "us"), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our equipment rental tracking and SMS/email reminder service.

Information We Collect

When you submit an equipment rental request or are added to our system as a field superintendent, we collect your name, mobile phone number, email address, and information about the equipment rentals associated with your jobsites.

How We Use Your Information

We use your mobile phone number and email address solely to send you reminders about active equipment rentals, upcoming rental due dates, and related job management updates. We do not use your information for marketing unrelated to this service.

SMS Messaging

By providing your mobile phone number, you consent to receive SMS text messages from SiteMind related to equipment rental reminders. Message frequency varies based on your active rentals — typically 1 message per day when you have active equipment, plus occasional alerts when a rental is nearing its end date. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, or rent your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number is used exclusively to deliver the equipment rental reminders described above.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the reminder service and to maintain accurate job and equipment records. You may request removal of your information at any time by contacting us.
